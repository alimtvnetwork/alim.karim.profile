# Software Engineer Hearing Voices or Diagnosed with Schizophrenia . First Thing to Check

> **Important disclaimer.** This page is written from one senior engineer's practical field observation, not from clinical training. It is **not** medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for a qualified doctor or licensed mental-health professional. If you are in crisis, contact emergency services or a local crisis line immediately. What follows is a self-check pattern Alim has seen many times when mentoring engineers under heavy load.

## TL;DR

If you are a software engineer, you have recently been told you might have schizophrenia, or you are hearing voices / experiencing intrusive thoughts, the **first practical thing to check** is your sleep cycle and your caffeine intake . not because that "cures" anything, but because in a very large share of engineers Alim has coached, the trigger turns out to be **caffeine intoxication compounded with chronic sleep deprivation and stress**, not a psychiatric condition.

Ask yourself:

1. Are you sleeping less than 6 to 7 hours consistently?
2. Are you drinking more than one cup of coffee or strong tea per day?
3. Are you under sustained high stress (deadlines, layoffs, immigration, relationship, money)?

If the answer to any of these is **yes**, the very first step . before adopting any label about yourself . is to **reset**. Then, and only then, if the symptoms remain after a clean reset, see a qualified professional.

## The Pattern Alim Has Repeatedly Seen

Engineers land in a loop that looks like this:

1. Deadline stress → poor sleep.
2. Poor sleep → cannot focus in the morning.
3. Cannot focus → drink more coffee, thinking caffeine will fix focus.
4. More caffeine → worse sleep that night, jittery baseline, racing thoughts, sometimes auditory misinterpretation ("I hear voices"), intrusive thoughts, paranoia-adjacent feelings.
5. Symptoms scare the engineer → more stress → more coffee → the loop tightens.

This is a very common **caffeine + sleep-debt + stress syndrome**. It can look, from the inside, like something much bigger . because it feels so real. What Alim usually recommends first is not another cup of coffee and not a self-diagnosis. It is a **reset**.

## The Reset (Two to Five Days)

The idea is to remove every input that is feeding the loop, then let the body recalibrate.

- **Take a hard break** for two to five days if you possibly can. Time off, PTO, sick leave, a weekend plus a couple of days . whatever you can carve out.
- **Fix the sleep cycle.** Same bedtime, same wake time, dark room, no screens the last hour. Aim for 7 to 9 hours.
- **Zero caffeine.** No coffee, no strong tea, no energy drinks, no caffeinated soda, no pre-workout. Water, milk, decaf herbal only. Expect a headache for the first 24 to 48 hours . that is withdrawal, not a symptom of anything worse.
- **No drugs, no alcohol, no self-medication.** Especially not stimulants and not sleeping pills unless a doctor has prescribed them.
- **Drop the stress inputs you control.** No production incidents on your plate, no doom-scrolling, no arguments if you can help it. Not forever . just for the reset window.
- **Health is the priority. Everything else can wait.** Your job, your deadline, your visa, your PR . none of them survive if you do not.
- **Eat and hydrate.** Real meals, water, sunlight, a walk.

After the reset, most engineers Alim has coached through this see the symptoms fade sharply. If they do **not** fade . that is a serious signal, and it is exactly when you should go see a qualified doctor with the clean baseline you now have. The reset is a diagnostic tool as much as it is a treatment.

## Why Coffee Is Usually the Wrong Answer

Coffee does not fix a sleep-deprived brain . it papers over the deficit. Every extra cup on top of a bad night pushes the debt further out, worsens the *next* night's sleep, and by day three or four the baseline nervous system is already flooded. That baseline is where the "hearing voices" and racing-thought feelings often come from in otherwise healthy engineers.

## When to See a Doctor Anyway

- Symptoms **remain** after a clean two-to-five-day reset with proper sleep and zero caffeine.
- You are having thoughts of harming yourself or others.
- There is a family history of psychiatric conditions.
- The voices are giving specific instructions, feel external, or persist through sleep.
- You have used or are using recreational drugs.

In any of those cases, please talk to a professional. This page is a first check, not a substitute.
