## The five pillars

1. **Spec-first** . no code without a machine-readable spec, routed to a named org-chart owner.
2. **Eval-gated** . every AI-generated PR must pass a retrieval / behavior / regression eval; the eval suite has a human owner.
3. **Guardrail-native** . prompt-injection, PII redaction, and output-schema validation are part of the pipeline, not bolted on.
4. **Cost-aware** . token cost, latency, and cache hit-rate are first-class SLOs; runaway prompts trip circuit breakers.
5. **Agent-augmented, human-owned** . agents draft; humans approve. The org chart decides who approves what.
