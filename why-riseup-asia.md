## The premium-not-cheap thesis

Most Asian sourcing channels optimise for **cost per hour**. That signal attracts the wrong engineers and creates a ceiling on architecture, ownership, and accountability. Riseup Asia LLC inverts the model:

- **Premium pay locally** so top Asian engineers can work with focus and dignity.
- **Premium pricing to the buyer** because the output is senior, accountable, and production-grade.
- **Premium output per dollar** compared with an H-1B or local senior hire under heavy US cost-of-living pressure.

This is the unique value proposition: **top 1% Asian engineering capacity without cheap-labor degradation**.

## The H-1B math

A US company paying **$10,000 per month** to an H-1B engineer in California, Los Angeles, or Texas may still be paying into rent, transport, taxes, healthcare, and basic cost-of-living pressure. The same budget routed through Riseup Asia LLC can fund a premium Asian engineer who has real domestic support, a dedicated workspace, and far less survival overhead.

That is why the company talks about engineers living like kings and serving like heroes: remove survival friction, add senior leadership, and the buyer gets more focus per dollar.
