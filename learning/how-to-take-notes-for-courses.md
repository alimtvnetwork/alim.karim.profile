## TL;DR

This companion session goes deeper on the *how* of note-taking: which apps to use, how to structure a course outline, how to mind-map on the fly, and what actually counts as note-taking (spoiler: copy-paste does not). Pair this with the [Mind Mapping & Memory Enhancement Training](./mind-mapping-memory-enhancement-training.md) for the full system.

## Who this is for

- Engineers taking online courses who want notes they can search years later.
- Freshers building their first learning system.
- Anyone who has abandoned Notion, Evernote, or OneNote three times and wants a workflow that finally sticks.

## Tools covered

1. **Notion** for structured course folders, outlines, and shareable pages.
2. **Workflowy** for outline-first mind mapping, focus in/out, and daily capture.
3. **HackMD** for markdown fluency and shareable technical notes.
4. **Xmind** and **GitMind** as visual mind-mapping companions.

## What you'll learn

- 📁 **Course scaffolding:** create a Notion workspace, sub-pages, and an outline page for any course (e.g. WordPress Elementor) in minutes.
- ⌨️ **Notion shortcuts:** use `/` to spawn Heading 1, Table of Contents, and class-day templates.
- 🌳 **Workflowy mind mapping:** capture thoughts as they arrive; perfect thoughts are a myth.
- 🔍 **Focus in / focus out:** zoom into a node, blur the rest, and beat context-switching.
- ✅ **Nodes as todos:** complete an item with `Ctrl + Enter`; treat every note as a work item.
- 🏷️ **Hashtags:** tag nodes for instant retrieval across huge outlines.
- 📤 **Sharing:** share a node by email or public link; export nodes cleanly.
- 📝 **Markdown basics in HackMD:** headings, lists, code blocks, and shareable URLs.
- 🚫 **What is NOT note-taking:** copy-paste with no summary; three or four of your own words beat a wall of pasted text.

## What counts as note-taking

- Note-taking is *learning the material* and keeping three or four words that pin it in your memory for the rest of your life.
- Copy-pasting without your own summary is **not** note-taking.
- After watching, apply the technique to [Rank and File (Real Life Magazine)](https://reallifemag.com/rank-and-file/) and mind-map the article.
